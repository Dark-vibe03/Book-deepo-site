Welcome to Books Deepo! To give you the best reading experience, we'd love to know your general location.

How we use your location:

If you grant permission, your exact GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) will be accessed and displayed in this reading experience.

Your Privacy Matters:

Location and camera access are optional for this demonstration. Your location and camera preview stay on this device. No photo or location is automatically uploaded to a server. You can deny permission at any time.
